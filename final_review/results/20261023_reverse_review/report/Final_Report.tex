\textbf{Research question.} Does selecting a prediction model for calibration,
rather than accuracy, improve the performance of an explicit sports-betting rule?
We implement the same research framework in a binary NBA market and a
three-outcome Premier League market, while preserving their different data,
selection dates and capital-allocation conventions.

\textbf{Main findings.} In the locked EPL experiment neither branch selects
an admissible validation trading rule, so both primary test policies hold
cash. Its calibration branch's secondary flat-stake yield is $0.41\%$, with
an interval crossing zero. New EPL player models do not improve matched
team controls. The reproducible NBA logistic account returns $0.98\%$ with
flat stakes and $-35.00\%$ with eighth-Kelly; separately restarted annual
logistic accounts have negative average returns for both objectives. The
source-reported SVM returns $25.03\%$ flat but $-55.11\%$ with Kelly; its
refit does not reproduce. Bankrupt-policy reversals are reported separately
as post-hoc sensitivity tests, not confirmed profitable policies.

\textbf{Evidence standard.} Original saved outputs are distinguished from
independent calculations. The NBA logistic replay reconciles to the supplied
bankrolls; the SVM refit does not, and its original numbers remain explicitly
source-reported. Historical profits are not presented as executable or
prospectively validated investment returns.

\section{NBA results}
\label{sec:nba}
\subsection{Data, features and selection roles}
The NBA component uses the supplied team-game CSV and season tables in
\texttt{OddsData.sqlite}. The CSV contains 33,316 team records and 16,658
distinct raw games across 2010/11--2023/24. After pairing teams and excluding
each club's first ten season appearances, 14,471 games remain; both teams
have at least ten earlier observations in each retained fixture.
The core betting season, 2018/19, contains 1,074 eligible games
with a complete matched moneyline pair. The saved 58.5\% home-win rate refers
to the training subset, not to all 14,471 games.

The initial feature set consists of 25 opponent-relative box-score statistics
and a previous-season win-rate difference. Shifted expanding means exclude the
target game. The training--validation KS screen removes offensive rebounds;
25 candidates remain. The Spearman screen retains nine variables. Forward
selection uses offensive rating and three-pointers made for the calibration
branch, and adds prior-season win percentage and turnovers for the accuracy
branch. These are different predictor subsets, not a single model scored two ways.

\resulttable{NBA chronology and sample sizes in the supplied core experiment.}
{tab:nba_splits}{nba_splits.tex}
There are 30 Optuna trials per family and branch. The source averages
predictions over ten random seeds for model-family assessment and the final
betting prediction. The season called \emph{test} in the notebook is used to
select the family: it is therefore a model-selection season in this report.
Only the subsequent 2018/19 betting season follows all these choices.

\resulttable{NBA model-family selection, 2017/18. The two columns describe
different branch-specific fitted variants; they are not joint scores of one model.}
{tab:nba_selection}{nba_selection.tex}
Calibration selection chooses logistic regression; accuracy selection chooses
the SVM. The calibration branch's selection ECE is $4.31\%$, while the
accuracy branch's selection accuracy is $66.33\%$. They must not be described
as the accuracy and ECE of a single predictor.

\subsection{The 2018/19 betting simulation}
Each native account starts with 10,000 monetary units. Flat staking requests
100 per qualifying game, equal to 1\% of \emph{initial} capital. One-eighth
Kelly varies the request with the predicted edge and available cash; unlike
the EPL rules, no additional 2\% per-bet ceiling is imposed. The simulator
checks home value before away value and settles games sequentially. Its
output labelled ``ROI'' is profit divided by starting capital. Table~\ref{tab:nba_capital}
uses the unambiguous term \emph{capital return}.

\resulttable{Original saved NBA betting results, 2018/19; initial capital 10,000.}
{tab:nba_capital}{nba_capital.tex}
\exhibit{nba_core_capital.pdf}{Ending capital of the four original saved NBA
2018/19 strategies. These are endpoint comparisons, not a reconstructed SVM
wealth path. LR = logistic regression. The SVM numbers are source-reported;
their supplied-parameter refit does not reconcile.}

The accuracy-selected SVM's positive flat-stake result and negative Kelly
result come from different exposure sizes, not different prediction models.
For the calibration-selected logistic model, flat stakes finish only slightly
above the starting account, while Kelly finishes substantially below it.
Thus the source evidence does not support treating calibration selection as
a guarantee of profitable stake sizing.

The saved betting-season prediction accuracy is $65.27\%$ for logistic
regression and $64.80\%$ for the SVM, distinct from their earlier selection
scores. The notebook's SVM calibration score is replaced by a bin-coverage
penalty; it is not an observation of literal 100\% calibration error.

\subsection{What the independent replay verifies}
A fixed-parameter audit uses the supplied feature subsets and stored
hyperparameters with ten-seed averaging. It performs no new feature search,
hyperparameter search or strategy optimisation. Both logistic account endings
match the notebook to the printed cent, and its saved accuracy and ordinary
20-bin ECE also reconcile. In contrast, the SVM refits reach the supplied
iteration limit and produce different predictions and bankrolls. Original
library versions and serialized fitted weights were not supplied. We retain
the original SVM results as \emph{reported} and do not silently replace them
with the mismatching refit.

\resulttable{Independently computed metrics on the same 1,074 NBA betting
fixtures. The logistic reconstruction reconciles; the bookmaker benchmark
is calculated directly from the provided odds.}
{tab:nba_verified_scores}{nba_verified_scores.tex}
The market probabilities have lower log loss and binary Brier loss and higher
accuracy than the reconciled logistic predictor. The logistic model, however,
has lower ordinary ECE in this season. We therefore do not claim that the
market dominates every metric in every season. In particular, a small global
calibration statistic does not establish a profitable advantage on the bets

For the same reconciled flat LR account, mean selected-outcome probability is 45.7821\% versus observed hit rate 37.9913\% (a 7.7909 percentage-point gap), despite ordinary global ECE 4.2721\%. Model-implied net payoff is 29.487 units/trade; realised net is 0.107. A small aggregate calibration error need not transfer to the price-selected bet subset.

\subsection{Nine independently restarted seasons}
The rolling extension contains nine betting seasons, 2014/15--2022/23, and
9,344 priced games. It reselects features in each window, but uses logistic
regression for \emph{both} branches. It does not repeat the core four-family
hyperparameter tournament. Each seasonal diagnostic restarts at 10,000 and
requests flat stakes of 100.

\resulttable{Saved NBA annual capital returns, flat 100 from a fresh 10,000
each season. Values are preserved at the source's two-decimal precision.}
{tab:nba_annual}{nba_annual.tex}
\exhibit{nba_annual_returns.pdf}{NBA annual capital returns for the rolling
logistic-only extension. Every bar represents a separate initial 10,000

The calibration branch has the higher return in only three of nine seasons.
Its reported mean return is $-17.34\%$ with sample standard deviation

replications establishing a precise long-run expected return.

The source's cumulative display adds cashflows from separately restarted

from this report's money-progression evidence. The supplied NBA write-up also
describes additional pooled market/fusion analyses whose underlying forecasts
are not preserved in the notebook. Those figures are not substituted for
the directly reconciled common-fixture comparison above.

\section{Premier League results}
\label{sec:epl}
\subsection{Data and adaptation to three outcomes}
The EPL component predicts home wins, draws and away wins. The focal universe
is the 20 saved 2026/27 Premier League clubs; historical opponents remain
context rather than being relabelled as current clubs. This present-day
cohort creates selection/survivor limitations. Its 2025/26 matched evaluation
contains 374 fixtures, rather than all 380 league fixtures.

Football-Data supplies results, team performance and individual-book odds;
Understat supplies team expected-goals histories; Fantasy Premier League
(FPL) supplies player records. The expanded player loader reconciles 253,509
historical player-match records in 2016/17--2025/26. The saved October 20
snapshot adds 4,551 current-season rows from 70 completed fixtures and 668
current player summaries. Historical inputs use strictly earlier dates;
target participants, target minutes and present-day rosters do not backfill
earlier forecasts. Role-based extensions disclose season-fixed position
proxies with unverified publication clocks.

\subsection{Proposal-aligned accuracy versus calibration experiment}
The first locked experiment considers the same four candidate families as the
NBA component, with 12 seeded tuning trials per family per branch. Earlier
training and tuning are followed by separate calibration, model-selection
and trading-validation seasons (Table~\ref{tab:league_method_differences}).
Both branches receive temperature scaling; the experiment compares selection
objectives rather than calibrated versus deliberately uncalibrated predictions.

Accuracy selection chooses an SVM using season-to-date corners difference
and recent shots-on-target difference. Calibration selection chooses an MLP
using earlier corners conceded. The original xG/player candidates do not
survive this small-budget selection path, so this experiment alone cannot
establish their incremental value.

\resulttable{Locked EPL 2025/26 probability scores on 374 identical fixtures.
ECE is mean classwise error with ten bins.}
{tab:epl_primary_scores}{epl_primary_scores.tex}
The calibration-selected MLP has lower ECE, log loss and Brier loss, but
lower classification accuracy, than the selected SVM. Normalised bookmaker
probabilities have stronger proper losses than either learned model. The
frequency baseline's low ECE despite poor log loss also illustrates the
limitations of using calibration error alone. Conditional paired intervals
for the MLP-minus-SVM ECE and log-loss differences both contain zero; they
omit model-selection uncertainty and do not establish a causal calibration effect.

Neither primary policy achieves positive validation profit with at least
30 validation bets. Both therefore select cash: zero test bets, zero
turnover and undefined stake ROI. This is the primary locked-policy finding.
The secondary 5\% edge rule is evaluated separately, with an original
1,000-unit account, flat 10 or capped quarter Kelly.

\resulttable{Secondary EPL 5\% edge replay on the test season; native initial
capital 1,000. Stake ROI is profit divided by turnover.}
{tab:epl_secondary}{epl_secondary.tex}
The MLP's best-price flat-stake ROI of $0.41\%$ is not robust evidence of an
edge: its interval crosses zero, its reference-price flat result is negative,
and its quarter-Kelly result is negative. Both primary rules remain in cash.
Later presentation accounts scale initial capital and fixed stakes by 100;
percentage ratios remain unchanged, while monetary amounts must be labelled
with their account basis.

\subsection{Exploratory team and player comparisons}
The rolling extension uses 1,470 common quoted fixtures in four seasons:
368 in 2022/23, 360 in 2023/24, 368 in 2024/25 and 374 in 2025/26. Each fit
uses earlier seasons for training, tuning and calibration. These are new
redesigns on already examined outcomes; chronology does not make them a fresh
confirmatory test.

The earlier source study crosses logistic and market-residual estimators
with team, team+xG, team+FPL and combined inputs. Market-residual models start
from normalised bookmaker probabilities and learn regularised feature
adjustments. The newer study compares logistic and histogram boosting on
matched team controls (19 inputs), player form (96), player form plus
projected XI/depth (222), and team plus the full player block (235).

\resulttable{Selected pooled EPL comparisons on the same 1,470 fixtures.
Best learned means lowest observed pooled log loss, not greatest trading capital.}
{tab:epl_extensions}{epl_rolling.tex}
The raw market has the best pooled proper scores among all 19 comparable
series. The strongest learned model uses bookmaker probabilities, so it is
not an odds-free team/player forecast. All six new player-based variants
have worse pooled log loss than their matched same-algorithm team controls.
This is a finding about these particular representations, not a general
claim that player information has no value.

\subsection{Prices, operational evidence and joint interpretation}
S060's profit is approximately 95,190 at the saved best quotes and 55,952
under a full Bet365 policy replay. Repricing its fixed original exposures
instead gives approximately $-30,549$. The latter is a payout attribution;
the former recomputes selections and cash sizes. These are different questions,
not a pure causal price effect. Quote capture clocks, accepted stake limits
and simultaneous fills remain unverified. Mathematical arbitrage flags do
not establish executed arbitrage or closing-line value.

The frozen four-season panel flags 4/1,470 mathematical arbitrage candidates (0.2721\%), with maximum theoretical margin 0.6272\%. Verified executable cases total 0; all 1,470 selected quote sets lack simultaneous clocks. The common 2025/26 panel has 0/374 candidates. These counts answer the arbitrage component without treating flags as earned profit.


The saved operational evidence has a separate October 20 cutoff: four earlier
models each classify six of ten resolved fixtures correctly. Forty earlier-model
and 80 new-model forecasts concern the same next ten October 23--25 fixtures.
This assembly performs no later result settlement, so these figures describe
the saved snapshot rather than live October 23 statuses. Missing pre-match
quotes and a trade ledger preclude prospective trading ROI.

\subsection{Opposite policies after bankruptcy: EPL diagnostic}
The 13 zero continuous main accounts comprise S041, S043 and 11 player
accounts. Four further zero reference-price replays of S033, S039, S041 and
S043 make 17 price contexts across 15 strategy IDs, not 17 independent
models. None of the 116 common-season accounts is zero. We also replay the
34 nonbankrupt main fixed-stake accounts as controls; the main comparison
therefore covers all 47 fixed-stake accounts, not only selected failures.

In a three-outcome market, ``opposite'' means covering both outcomes other
than the original pick, including the draw. With archived decimal odds
$O_k$, $A=\sum_{k\ne s}1/O_k$, and total risk request $B=1,000$, we allocate
$B/(O_kA)$ to each complementary outcome. This equalises gross payout at
$B/A$; it does not stake 1,000 on each leg. The synthetic odds $1/A$ can be
at or below one, so event occurrence and positive trading profit are not
identical. Expected gain/loss sum probability-weighted positive/negative
payoffs over all three outcomes, including these loss-making bundles.

We keep the frozen original signal and its entry filter, continue through
all 1,470 fixtures after original ruin, and do not impose a new value test
on the opposite outcome. Each account starts at 100,000; same-date risks
are proportionally scaled to opening cash before settlement, without
borrowing or reusing same-date proceeds. One thousand is 1\% of initial
capital, not a constant fraction of current cash.

\resulttable{Full-horizon EPL opposite policies from 100,000, main cohort
only. ``Profit'' counts ending balances above 100,000. Costs haircut each
fixture's positive net profit by 0\%, 2\% or 5\%; they are scenarios, not
observed venue commissions.}{tab:epl_inverse_controls}{epl_inverse_controls.tex}

At zero added cost, 2 of 13 bankrupt-original complements finish above 100,000, compared with 2 of 34 nonbankrupt controls. These are descriptive, correlated outcomes, not an adjusted hypothesis test. P009's original account ends at zero; its priced complement ends at 92,927, makes 1,384 funded trades from 1,384 frozen signals, and has 29.63\% maximum drawdown. Its conditional stake-yield interval is [-3.60\%, 2.60\%]; these whole-date cashflow intervals omit refitting, resizing and the bankruptcy-based selection.

Across all 17 zero price contexts, the quoted complements profit in 2/17 cases, with median ending capital 62,010 and range 0--116,968. The synthetic same-back-price lay profits in 16/17 cases, with median 140,580. The apparent improvement depends on a different, unobserved price contract; it cannot be attributed to changing only the direction of the bet. No zero-cost priced complement has a conditional 95\% stake-yield interval entirely above zero.


A second calculation negates the \emph{recorded original cash flows}; every
100,000-to-zero history then maps to 200,000 by algebra. This truncated-path
identity is not the funded complement. A separate synthetic lay fixes
liability at 1,000 and uses counterstake $1,000/(O_s-1)$ at the original
back quote. It requires unavailable lay-price assumptions and is not an
exchange backtest. Both are labelled diagnostics. The actual complementary
bookmaker quotes lack verified simultaneous availability or accepted stakes.
Bankruptcy prompted these rules; no inverse is promoted as new confirmed
alpha or substituted for the original locked cash policy.

\subsection{Final qualitative and quantitative assessment}
The shared question is answered by the matched comparisons, not by the
largest ex-post account. The original NBA flat capital return favours
accuracy selection by 24.05 percentage points (with the SVM reproduction
limit); eighth-Kelly favours calibration by 20.11 points while both lose.
Across nine annual LR windows calibration wins only three times, with mean
returns of $-17.34\%$ versus $-9.49\%$. EPL primary policies both hold cash;
the secondary flat calibration yield exceeds accuracy by 2.66 points, but
its interval includes zero. Better observed ECE therefore does not establish
a durable advantage in betting returns. Binary/three-outcome scores,
capital returns/stake yields and annual/continuous accounts are not pooled.

The EPL raw market remains the strongest pooled proper-score benchmark,
and all six added player variants lose to their matched team controls on
log loss. Of 88 continuous exploratory accounts, 24 profit and 64 lose,
including 13 bankrupt; the best account's conditional interval includes
zero. For the original 84-strategy family only, the saved global bootstrap
p-values range from 0.3887--0.4187 versus cash and 0.1457--0.1952 versus
the market-favourite baseline across block lengths 1, 3, 5 and 10. These
conditional recorded-cashflow tests do not adjust the newer player or
inverse families, and no family supplies established profitable alpha.

The strongest contributions are the implemented cross-league comparison,
shifted feature construction, explicit funding, market benchmarks and
auditable negative findings. The remaining limits are narrow outcome and
season samples, the current-club EPL cohort, approximate/inconsistent
source environments, unavailable quote clocks, and post-hoc strategy
expansion. Calibration describes probability reliability; betting also
depends on prices, the selected subset, costs and exposure. Opposite-policy
successes, where observed, test the direction of failed signals and are
exploratory. A decisive extension would precommit the model, entry rule,
stake budget and evaluation horizon before collecting a fresh cohort of
timestamped available quotes. The study supports this discipline rather
than a claim of implementable statistical arbitrage.

not deposits into a continuous account. Model expected gains and losses are
probability-weighted components at the recorded stakes, not average

\subsection{Risk and payoff rules}
All EPL inverses use the original frozen qualification over the full
1,470-fixture horizon. Complement requests split a total 1,000 between the
two alternative outcomes in reciprocal-odds proportions. Synthetic lay
requests reserve total liability 1,000 and compute the counterstake from
the back quote; they do not assume observed exchange liquidity. Risks are
scaled together to date-start cash. Cost sensitivity applies to a fixture's
positive \emph{net bundle profit}, with no rebate on losses, and cash sizes
are replayed for each scenario. Thus the additional haircut does not double
count a winning leg while ignoring its losing complementary leg. Bookmaker
vig is already embedded in the archived odds.

Hit rate reports occurrence of the complementary event. A bundle can still
lose if its synthetic odds do not exceed one; the CSV also preserves the
fraction of bundles earning positive net profit. Expected components use
the original forecast probabilities and signed outcome payoffs, not the
empirical frequency of wins. The original model need not endorse its
contrarian trade. Full CSVs additionally contain turnover, realised payoff
components, funding checks and source/context identifiers.
