# Premier League betting data: source audit

Checked against provider documentation and public pages on September 24–25, 2026. This is an availability and design audit, not a downloaded-data coverage audit. No account was created, subscription purchased, or wager placed. A direct public Kalshi API probe was blocked by local network restrictions; an escalation was not executed because automatic approval review encountered a service usage limit. Consequently, historical coverage, missingness, and executable depth still require a small pilot download.

## Minimum information needed before claiming a tradable result

For each observation retain the fixture ID, competition, home/away teams, scheduled and revised kickoff in UTC, exact market definition, selection, quoted price, quote side, size if observed, source timestamp, retrieval timestamp, and market status. Keep outcomes and settlement rules in separate fields. For player data, also store player/team IDs, fixture played, minutes/statistics, source publication time or conservative availability cutoff, and lineup publication time if used.

Map regular-time home/draw/away outcomes across venues. Home-win NO includes both draw and away win. Void, postponement, abandoned-match and correction policies can differ even when ordinary outcomes match. Cross-venue payoff equivalence requires those cases to be reconciled; otherwise classify the position as basis risk, not locked-in arbitrage.

Synchronize with backward as-of joins using observations actually available at the decision time. Reject stale quotes under a rule set before the test. Keep missing fixtures and unavailable books in the coverage report. Do not filter on a game's eventual volume or full-match realized liquidity when selecting it earlier in time.

## Practical acquisition choices

1. **Intraday study:** synchronized sportsbook snapshots plus one prediction venue with historical bid/ask data, supplemented by a prospective book recorder. A target season is a requested sample, not confirmed coverage. Establish overlap and budget before scaling downloads.
2. **Without paid historical sportsbook data:** use public historical bookmaker data for a separate, slower hold-to-settlement baseline and collect a modest prospective synchronized sample at explicitly recorded decision times. Report the dynamic component as a pilot if the sample stays small. A closing-only dataset cannot stand in for the missing intraday experiment.
3. **Optional second execution venue:** add Polymarket or Betfair only after the first venue is working. A second venue is not required to demonstrate the core statistical method.

These findings inform the project scope. They do not establish an arbitrage opportunity, forecast accuracy, strategy return, or statistical significance.
