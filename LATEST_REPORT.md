second and Premier League results last. It now includes funded opposite-policy
experiments for all 19 original zero-ending account contexts, a final qualitative
and quantitative assessment, cost sensitivities and the complete original strategy
appendix. The presentation has 15 main slides plus 13 appendix slides.

The NBA opposites end at 11,148.07 and 8,785.30 from 10,000. Priced EPL
complements profit in only 2/17 bankrupt strategy/price contexts; their median
ends at 62,009.54 from 100,000. Hypothetical same-price lays are shown separately:
the required lay quotes were not observed. Neither profitable EPL inverse has
a conditional interval excluding zero. These post-hoc studies do not establish
a reliable future edge.
