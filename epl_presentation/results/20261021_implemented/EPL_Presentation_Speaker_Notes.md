## Slide 15: The same-scope trading scorecard

All rows: continuous four seasons, 1,470 priced opportunities, one initial 100,000 account.

The scorecard intentionally aligns all six featured policies to the same four-season scope. Expected gain per trade averages p times stake times odds minus one; expected loss averages one minus p times stake. These may look favorable even when actual trading loses because they rely on the model's probabilities, especially on selected high-edge bets. ROI divides net profit by turnover and is not return on starting capital. Frequency divides actual placed bets by all 1470 common priced opportunities; bankruptcy can reduce realized frequency. The companion workbook also preserves average observed winning/losing payoffs and the confidence intervals.

## Slide 19: Backup | metric definitions

All money values are generic monetary units. Cash accounts have no deposits or borrowing.

Expected components use the selected model probability at the recorded allocated stake. They are unconditional components per trade, not the conditional mean payoff of wins or losses. No-trade conditional quantities remain undefined in the source workbook. Log loss is negative mean log probability assigned to the observed H/D/A outcome; Brier is the unscaled three-class squared error. ECE is classwise calibration error, bin-dependent and descriptive. Cash stays flat when no interest is modeled. Conditional date-bootstrap intervals are not a full model-and-search uncertainty analysis.
